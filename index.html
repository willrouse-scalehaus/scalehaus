The Seven Leaks

We find the hole. We seal it.

Every finding maps to one of seven leaks. The order is the priority — fix the top first, prove it, then move to the next.

01
Speed
Inquiry at 8pm, reply next morning. Interest decayed — they called a competitor by 9am. Fix: auto-respond within 5 seconds, human within 5 minutes.
02
Capture
Traffic arrives, nobody raises a hand. No visible way to start, no reason to now. Fix: one clear CTA per screen. Self-scheduling. Click-to-text.
03
Message
Generic claims — "state-of-the-art," "top-rated." Says nothing the buyer was actually searching for. Fix: answer the real question — "Can you fix MY problem? What will it look like? How much?"
04
Clarity
Competing buttons, invisible offer, no price context. Buyer cannot picture buying — so they don't. Fix: one primary action per view. Visible starting price. Clear hierarchy.
05
Proof
Stock photos. Thin reviews. No real work shown. Trust is earned, not claimed. Fix: real results with documented consent. Verified reviews. Case studies with context.
06
Ad quality
Wrong audience, wrong creative, clicks landing on a homepage instead of the offer. Fix: single-offer landing pages. Track every click to source.
07
Sequence
One call, then silence. No nurture, no no-show recovery, no reactivation. Fix: structured follow-up sequence. Every lead gets three touches in 24 hours.

FAQ

Questions we hear.

What if my market is already taken? +
We tell you immediately. We take one business per metro area per vertical. If your competitor already works with us, we will not take your money. We will offer to put you on the waitlist if the territory opens.
I already work with an agency. What makes you different? +
Most agencies sell you traffic and hope. We find the leak first, seal it, prove it, and only then pour fuel. If your current agency is delivering attributable revenue, stay with them. If they are delivering lead counts and hoping, that is not the same thing.
How long does it take to see results? +
The audit takes 48 hours. The first fix is implemented within one week of starting. Most clients see measurable improvement in the first 30 days. The full system — find, seal, fuel — typically takes 60–90 days depending on the complexity of the largest constraint.
Do you guarantee results? +
No. We do not guarantee a revenue outcome we do not fully control. What we guarantee is in writing: the work, the measurement, the accountability. Every fix is measured before and after. If a fix does not improve the metric, we do not bill for it. That is the guarantee.
What does the audit cost? +
Nothing. The audit is free. It is the first step of the system — we do not charge to diagnose a problem we have not proven exists. If the economics do not work for your business, we tell you honestly and part ways. No invoice, no hard feelings.
