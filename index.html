The Seven Leaks

We find the hole. We seal it.

Every finding maps to one of seven leaks. The order is the priority — fix the top first, prove it, then move to the next.

01
Speed
Inquiry at 8pm, reply next morning. Interest decayed — they called a competitor by 9am. Fix: auto-respond within 5 seconds, human within 5 minutes.
02
Capture
Traffic arrives, nobody raises a hand. No visible way to start, no reason to now. Fix: one clear CTA per screen. Self-scheduling. Click-to-text.
03
Message
Generic claims — "state-of-the-art," "top-rated." Says nothing the patient was actually searching for. Fix: answer the real question — "Can you fix MY problem? What will it look like? How much?"
04
Clarity
Competing buttons, invisible offer, no price context. Patient can't picture buying — so they don't. Fix: one primary action per view. Visible starting price. Clear hierarchy.
05
Proof
Stock photos. Thin reviews. No real work shown. Trust is earned, not claimed. Fix: real patient photos with consent. Verified reviews. Before/afters with context.
06
Ad quality
Wrong audience, wrong creative, clicks landing on a homepage instead of the offer. Fix: single-offer landing pages. Track every click to source.
07
Sequence
One call, then silence. No nurture, no no-show recovery, no reactivation. Fix: structured follow-up sequence. Every lead gets three touches in 24 hours.
